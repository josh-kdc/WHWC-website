Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Walking Home Wellness Coaching ("I," "me," or "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit walkinghomewellness.com (the "Site") or use its contact and scheduling features.

Information I Collect

I collect information in a few limited ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, and any message content when you submit the contact form.
  • Scheduling information — when you book a session through Calendly, Calendly collects the details needed to schedule (name, email, and appointment preferences). This is governed by Calendly's own privacy policy.
  • Analytics data — anonymized usage data (such as pages visited and general location) collected through Google Analytics, but only if you consent via the cookie banner on the Site.

I do not collect payment information, health records, or clinical data through this website. Any information shared during coaching sessions themselves is governed separately by the confidentiality terms outlined in my coaching agreement, not this website policy.

Cookies

This Site uses cookies to support basic functionality and, with your consent, to understand how visitors use the Site. When you first visit, you'll see a banner asking you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Google Analytics only begins collecting data if you choose "Accept." You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this Site and reloading the page.

No cookies on this Site are used for advertising or to sell your information to third parties.

How I Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form
  • To schedule and manage coaching sessions
  • To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Site so I can improve it

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This Site relies on a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Calendly — handles session scheduling. See Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymized site usage insights, only with your consent. See Google's privacy policy.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to the personal information I hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that information
  • Withdraw consent for analytics cookies at any time
  • Opt out of having your information shared, where applicable (California residents)

To exercise any of these rights, contact me using the information below.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions and scheduling information are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or provide coaching services, unless you request earlier deletion.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in the Site or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
